Payment orders

TEST MODE: orders are test orders and no real money moves. A plan confirms only when the server processes a verified payment event.

Gift credit

Adds money to a customer's balance as a labelled line. It earns returns on the plan and term you pick. All choices come from the Products and Choices tabs.

Withdrawal requests

Accepting closes the plan and removes it from the customer's balance. This system sends NO money. Pay the customer separately. Rejecting puts the plan back to normal.

Plan requests

Setting CONFIRMED by hand is for testing only. In production, only a verified payment event may confirm a plan. For a gift, REJECTED reverses it.
